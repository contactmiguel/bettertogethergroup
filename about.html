THREE DECADES IN THE ROOM

Strategic counsel for the moments that define leadership.

For 30 years, Claudia Beck, MCC has sat beside the world's most influential leaders, navigating the complex intersection of high-stakes strategy and human behavior.

THE STORY

"I realized early on that the best strategy in the world fails if the culture isn't ready to receive it."

It began in rooms where the air felt heavy with the weight of global decisions. As a young advisor, I watched brilliant strategies dissolve into thin air because of unspoken tensions, misaligned incentives, or a simple lack of trust at the top.

I spent twenty years in the trenches of corporate transformation before I realized my true calling wasn't just fixing processes—it was helping the humans behind those processes find their collective voice.

Founding Better Together

Better Together Group was founded on a singular conviction: that strategy fails not on the whiteboard, but in the room — in the unspoken tensions, misaligned incentives, and quiet breakdowns of trust that no slide deck can fix. The practice is built around named IP, a rigorous engagement model, and direct access to me as principal.

Engagements are selective, not templated. I enter each one with the humility of an outsider and the commitment of a stakeholder — listening for what isn't being said, and naming it when it matters.

OUR PROPRIETARY FRAMEWORK

The A.T.T.U.N.E.™ Framework

After three decades, I codified the six dimensions that shape how leaders perform under pressure: Awareness, Tone, Trust, Understanding, Naming, and Equilibrium. Every individual engagement is anchored in this framework. The team application lives on the ATTUNE Leadership site.
