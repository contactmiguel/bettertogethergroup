## SECTION 2 — THE STORY

*Design: Light background. First person. Conversational but precise — matches how Claudia actually writes.*

> I spent thirty years inside the organizations I now advise.
>
> Senior operating roles at Burberry, Tory Burch, Gap Inc., and PwC. Revenue accountability. Global teams. Five countries — the United States, the United Kingdom, China, Japan, and Austria. I worked in environments where strategy, power, and personality collide daily, and where the gap between what was said in a meeting and what was actually true could determine whether a business moved or stalled.
>
> I was good at results. What I learned over time was that results are downstream of something else entirely — the quality of the decisions being made, the trust in the room where they were made, and the conversations that were happening or consistently not happening.
>
> That insight became the foundation of my advisory practice. And eventually, the foundation of the A.T.T.U.N.E.™ Leadership Framework.
