### CASE 01

**Title (large display type, navy):**
> When Reliability Stops Scaling

**ATTUNE dimensions (gold pills):**
> `Awareness` `Trust` `Naming`

**The situation:**
> A highly trusted Vice President — reliable, delivery-focused, consistently high-performing — found that the behaviors driving his success were now limiting the organization around him. As his scope expanded, his tendency to carry responsibility personally had begun eroding team ownership. Difficult conversations were being deferred. Execution was still happening, but through him rather than because of him.

**The shift:**
> From high-performing executor to enterprise leader who builds outcomes through others — through distributed accountability, earlier feedback, and delegation at the outcome level rather than the task level.

**The outcome:**
> Team ownership increased. Performance conversations became more direct and timely. Cross-regional alignment improved. He was promoted to Senior Vice President.

### CASE 02

**Title (large display type, navy):**
> When Expertise Stops Scaling

**ATTUNE dimensions (gold pills):**
> `Awareness` `Tone` `Understanding`

**The situation:**
> A senior data executive at a global energy company — technically brilliant, industry-recognized, with growing enterprise visibility — was navigating a familiar transition: from being the expert who drives outcomes to the leader who multiplies impact through others. Under pressure, impatience surfaced. Conversations became reactive. Collaboration began to suffer despite strong individual performance.

**The shift:**
> From technical authority to enterprise influence — through recognizing emotional triggers earlier, asking before advising, and allowing others to own decisions and solutions fully.

**The outcome:**
> Team collaboration improved. Cross-functional relationships strengthened. His influence expanded across the organization as leaders looked to him not just for answers, but for alignment.

### CASE 03

**Title (large display type, navy):**
> When Pressure Distorts Executive Judgment

**ATTUNE dimensions (gold pills):**
> `Awareness` `Equilibrium` `Understanding`

**The situation:**
> A senior leader running a 250-person global organization during a shift from growth to optimization. Risk tolerance had dropped, political scrutiny had increased, and she had begun over-preparing, over-interpreting threat, and leaving executive meetings exhausted. Performance remained strong. Confidence was quietly eroding — not from lack of skill, but because protecting what she had built had begun to dominate her attention.

**The shift:**
> From threat-driven to grounded strategic authority — through separating real organizational risk from political noise, distinguishing structural volatility from personal exposure, and recalibrating what was actually hers to carry.

**The outcome:**
> Executive contributions became direct and decisive. Preparation time materially reduced. Energy stabilized. Physical wellbeing improved. Scope and compensation were preserved through the optimization phase.

**Client's words:**
> *"My anxiety wasn't about competence. It was fear. When I manage my emotional patterns and choose intentionally, everything shifts."*
