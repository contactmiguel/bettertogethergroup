## SECTION 2 — WHAT IT IS

*Design: Light background. Single column prose. Confirms the visitor is in the right place.*

> At a certain level of seniority, the most consequential decisions are also the hardest to think through clearly inside the organization. The political dynamics are too present. The stakes are too high. The need to appear decided too strong.
>
> Decision Architecture advisory is built for that moment.
>
> It is a confidential, structured partnership between Claudia and one senior leader. The work is organized around the decisions, dynamics, and pressures that are creating the most friction — not a standard agenda, not a fixed program. What gets worked on is what the leader is actually navigating.
>
> The A.T.T.U.N.E.™ framework operates as the diagnostic lens throughout: surfacing what is being avoided, what is going unsaid, and where the leader's own patterns are shaping the outcomes around them.

## SECTION 3 — WHAT GETS WORKED ON

*Design: Navy background. Four numbered cards — each a specific situation the right visitor will recognise.*

**Section label (small caps, gold):**
> WHAT THE WORK ADDRESSES

**01 — Decision quality under pressure**
> When the weight of consequential decisions becomes isolating — the financial, reputational, or organizational stakes are high and the leader needs a confidential space to think clearly before acting.

**02 — Executive influence and credibility**
> When a role expands faster than internal confidence can keep pace — and the leader needs to build the presence, communication, and relational authority the new scope requires.

**03 — Cross-functional dynamics**
> When friction between peers, stakeholders, or the team is creating execution drag — and the source is relational rather than structural.

**04 — Leadership under sustained scrutiny**
> When elevated visibility, a difficult stakeholder dynamic, or a sustained period of organizational pressure is beginning to distort judgment or erode clarity.

## SECTION 4 — HOW IT WORKS

*Design: Light background. Three clean phases.*

**Section label (small caps, gold):**
> THE STRUCTURE

**Intake**
> The engagement begins with a structured intake — understanding the leader's situation, the decisions they are navigating, and what the partnership needs to achieve. The work is shaped from there, not from a template.

**Ongoing Sessions**
> Regular one-to-one sessions with Claudia, structured around the leader's live priorities. Sessions move from the presenting situation into the dynamics beneath it — and end in clarity, not just in conversation.

**Between Sessions**
> The work does not stop when the session ends. Leaders are supported in the moments when decisions need to be made, not just when meetings are scheduled.

## SECTION 5 — WHO THIS IS FOR

*Design: Light grey background. Self-selection criteria — specific enough that the right person immediately recognises their situation.*

**Section label (small caps, gold):**
> IS THIS THE RIGHT FIT?

> Senior leaders engage this work when:

- The weight of responsibility has become quietly isolating — and the honest conversations are harder to find inside the organization
- A role has expanded in scope, visibility, or consequence faster than the internal support structures have kept pace
- A significant decision is approaching that carries financial, reputational, or organizational risk
- A cross-functional relationship or team dynamic is creating friction that is beginning to affect performance
- They need a confidential space to think clearly, examine their own patterns, and act with more precision

> This work is for leaders who carry real consequence and are willing to examine how their own decisions — and their own dynamics — shape the outcomes around them.
