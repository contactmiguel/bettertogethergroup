## SECTION 2 — WHAT IT IS

*Design: Light background. Three short paragraphs. The second sentence of the first paragraph is pulled as a visual pull quote — breaks the prose before it gets heavy.*

> Most leadership retreats end with a shared meal, a slide deck summarizing what was discussed, and good intentions that dissolve within a fortnight.

**Pull quote (centered, gold bars above and below):**
> *"A Bespoke Retreat is not a planning session. It is not a team-building event. It is a structured behavioral working retreat."*

> Two days in which a leadership team confronts the patterns that slow execution, rebuilds the trust that performance depends on, and makes the commitments that change how it operates going forward.
>
> The work is grounded in The Five Behaviors framework and shaped throughout by the A.T.T.U.N.E.™ diagnostic lens. The combination surfaces both the structural and the relational: what the team has agreed to do, and what has been getting in the way of doing it.

## SECTION 4 — THE ARC OF THE RETREAT

*Design: Light background. NOT a list of prose phases. An illustrated arc graphic — a curve that rises from "Before" on the left, peaks at "Day 2," and lands at "After" on the right. Each phase sits on the arc with a label and one sentence.*

*Animation: The arc draws itself left to right as the visitor scrolls into the section. Each phase dot activates sequentially with a gold pulse, then the label and sentence appear. Total animation: ~2 seconds.*

*Day 1 and Day 2 are visually distinct: Day 1 in deeper navy (going inward, building trust foundations), Day 2 in a lighter navy-blue (emerging, setting direction). The visual contrast shows the shift in the work.*

**Section label (small caps, gold):**
> HOW IT UNFOLDS

**Before** *(arc start — left)*
> Diagnostic assessments and intake conversations. The agenda is built from what is found — not from a template.

**Day One** *(arc rising — deeper navy)*
> Trust and productive conflict. The conversations that have been deferred are structured and held. The relational foundation is built.

**Day Two** *(arc peak — lighter navy-blue)*
> Commitment, accountability, and results. How decisions are made, how accountability is held, and what changes in how the team operates going forward.

**After** *(arc landing — right)*
> Documented commitments and agreed operating norms. A follow-up conversation at 30 and 90 days to assess what has held.

## SECTION 5 — WHAT CHANGES

*Design: White background. Two-column card layout — matches Intensives page format for visual consistency. Before column: text is muted/faded on load. After column: text fades in at full strength in navy on scroll — a subtle reveal that visually enacts the transformation.*

**Section label (small caps, gold):**
> WHAT CHANGES

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Trust is assumed rather than examined | The team has explicitly named where trust holds and where it has been absent |
| Difficult conversations are managed around, not through | The team has demonstrated it can have the hard conversation and remain intact |
| The same patterns keep surfacing in different forms | The behavioral root has been named and addressed, not just managed |
| Accountability is personal, not collective | The team has shared operating norms and explicit mutual commitments |
| The offsite energy fades within weeks | Commitments are documented and followed up at 30 and 90 days |

## SECTION 6 — WHO THIS IS FOR

*Design: Light grey background. Five scenario cards in a 2+2+1 grid. Each card: one Tabler outline icon (gold, 24px) + one sentence. No bullet points — cards are scannable in seconds.*

**Section label (small caps, gold):**
> IS THIS THE RIGHT FIT?

**Card 5 centered** *(icon: ti-plant — foundation)*
