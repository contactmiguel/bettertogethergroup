<div>
<span class="font-display-lg text-[64px] text-metallic-gold mb-2 block leading-none">A</span>
<h3 class="font-headline-sm text-headline-sm text-stark-white mb-stack-sm group-hover:text-metallic-gold transition-colors">Awareness</h3>
<p class="font-body-md text-body-md text-on-surface-variant mb-stack-lg"><span class="block font-semibold mb-2">Self-regulation before response</span><strong>Leaders who react from unexamined triggers lose authority faster than those who pause.</strong><br><br>Every high-stakes conversation is shaped by what the leader carries into it — unresolved pressure, competing priorities, fear that has not been named. Awareness is the discipline of noticing that before it shapes what happens in the room.<br><br>Strong reactions are rarely about the moment. They almost always signal something beneath it.</p>
</div>
<div>
<p class="font-quote text-quote italic text-metallic-gold/80 mb-4">"What am I carrying into this conversation that does not belong here?"</p>
<div class="w-12 h-px bg-metallic-gold/40"></div>
</div>

<!-- Tone -->
<div class="border-r border-b border-metallic-gold/20 p-stack-lg min-h-[400px] flex flex-col justify-between hover:bg-deep-navy/50 transition-colors group">
<div>
<span class="font-display-lg text-[64px] text-metallic-gold mb-2 block leading-none">T</span>
<h3 class="font-headline-sm text-headline-sm text-stark-white mb-stack-sm group-hover:text-metallic-gold transition-colors">Tone</h3>
<p class="font-body-md text-body-md text-on-surface-variant mb-stack-lg"><span class="block font-semibold mb-2">How you communicate shapes every relationship</span><strong>The emotional tone you carry enters the room before you speak a word.</strong><br><br>Regulated leaders build relationship capital. Reactive leaders spend it faster than they earn it. Tone is not about warmth or formality — it is about whether the climate you create makes people more or less able to think clearly, speak honestly, and act with confidence.<br><br>Communication that is calm and direct builds the relational foundation that execution depends on.</p>
</div>
<div>
<p class="font-quote text-quote italic text-metallic-gold/80 mb-4">"What is my tone communicating right now — and is it creating clarity or noise?"</p>
<div class="w-12 h-px bg-metallic-gold/40"></div>
</div>
</div>
<!-- Trust -->
<div class="border-r border-b border-metallic-gold/20 p-stack-lg min-h-[400px] flex flex-col justify-between hover:bg-deep-navy/50 transition-colors group">
<div>
<span class="font-display-lg text-[64px] text-metallic-gold mb-2 block leading-none">T</span>
<h3 class="font-headline-sm text-headline-sm text-stark-white mb-stack-sm group-hover:text-metallic-gold transition-colors">Trust</h3>
<p class="font-body-md text-body-md text-on-surface-variant mb-stack-lg"><span class="block font-semibold mb-2">The architecture of honest conversation</span><strong>Speed of execution is directly proportional to the level of trust in the room.</strong><br><br>Leaders who avoid difficult conversations believe they are protecting the relationship. They are not. They are deferring its erosion. The absence of honest conversation does not preserve trust — it quietly drains it, until the moment when the drain becomes visible and the cost is already high.<br><br>Trust is not built through agreement. It is built through the willingness to say the hard thing and remain in the relationship afterward.</p>
</div>
<div>
<p class="font-quote text-quote italic text-metallic-gold/80 mb-4">"Are people able to tell me the hard thing? If not — why not?"</p>
<div class="w-12 h-px bg-metallic-gold/40"></div>
</div>
</div>
<!-- Understanding -->
<div class="border-r border-b border-metallic-gold/20 p-stack-lg min-h-[400px] flex flex-col justify-between hover:bg-deep-navy/50 transition-colors group">
<div>
<span class="font-display-lg text-[64px] text-metallic-gold mb-2 block leading-none">U</span>
<h3 class="font-headline-sm text-headline-sm text-stark-white mb-stack-sm group-hover:text-metallic-gold transition-colors">Understanding</h3>
<p class="font-body-md text-body-md text-on-surface-variant mb-stack-lg"><span class="block font-semibold mb-2">The quality of listening shapes every relationship</span><strong>The presenting issue is rarely the real issue.</strong><br><br>What goes unread beneath behavior — the fear, the misalignment, the pressure that has no outlet — becomes political drag. It surfaces in side conversations, passive resistance, decisions that seem inexplicable until you understand what the person making them was actually responding to.<br><br>The most expensive leadership blind spot is mistaking silence for agreement. Relationships erode in that gap.</p>
</div>
<div>
<p class="font-quote text-quote italic text-metallic-gold/80 mb-4">"What is the real issue underneath what I am being told?"</p>
<div class="w-12 h-px bg-metallic-gold/40"></div>
</div>
</div>
<!-- Naming -->
<div class="border-r border-b border-metallic-gold/20 p-stack-lg min-h-[400px] flex flex-col justify-between hover:bg-deep-navy/50 transition-colors group">
<div>
<span class="font-display-lg text-[64px] text-metallic-gold mb-2 block leading-none">N</span>
<h3 class="font-headline-sm text-headline-sm text-stark-white mb-stack-sm group-hover:text-metallic-gold transition-colors">Naming</h3>
<p class="font-body-md text-body-md text-on-surface-variant mb-stack-lg"><span class="block font-semibold mb-2">Clarity as a leadership act</span><strong>Unspoken dynamics do not disappear. They surface as disengagement, politics, or poor decisions.</strong><br><br>Naming what others avoid is not a confrontation — it is the communication act that builds the deepest professional trust. The leaders who can say the hard thing clearly, without blame, and without withdrawing from the relationship afterward accelerate every process around them.<br><br>What the room cannot name, the room cannot resolve.</p>
</div>
<div>
<p class="font-quote text-quote italic text-metallic-gold/80 mb-4">"What truth in this room am I avoiding naming — and what is that costing?"</p>
<div class="w-12 h-px bg-metallic-gold/40"></div>

<!-- Equilibrium -->
<div class="border-r border-b border-metallic-gold/20 p-stack-lg min-h-[400px] flex flex-col justify-between hover:bg-deep-navy/50 transition-colors group">
<div>
<span class="font-display-lg text-[64px] text-metallic-gold mb-2 block leading-none">E</span>
<h3 class="font-headline-sm text-headline-sm text-stark-white mb-stack-sm group-hover:text-metallic-gold transition-colors">Equilibrium</h3>
<p class="font-body-md text-body-md text-on-surface-variant mb-stack-lg"><span class="block font-semibold mb-2">Stability under pressure</span><strong>Equilibrium is not the absence of pressure. It is the capacity to lead clearly inside it.</strong><br><br>Under sustained pressure, even skilled leaders begin to lead from the pressure rather than through it. Decisions become defensive. Preparation becomes excessive. The instinct that was once an asset begins to override judgment.<br><br>Relationships survive difficulty not because conflict is avoided, but because repair is done well. The response to a difficult moment is always more visible — and more consequential — than the moment itself.</p>
</div>
<div>
<p class="font-quote text-quote italic text-metallic-gold/80 mb-4">"Am I leading from equilibrium right now — or from the pressure itself?"</p>

            const target = 6000;

            let start = 0;
            const startTime = performance.now();

            const update = (currentTime) => {

                // Ease out cubic
                const ease = 1 - Math.pow(1 - progress, 3);
                const current = Math.floor(ease * target);
