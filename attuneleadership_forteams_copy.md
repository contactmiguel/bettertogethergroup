## SECTION 3 — THE APPROACH

**Section label (small caps, gold):**
> THE APPROACH

> The same six dimensions that surface an individual leader's blind spots surface a leadership team's collective ones.
>
> Awareness. Tone. Trust. Understanding. Naming. Equilibrium.
>
> Applied to a team, each dimension becomes a question about how the group operates under pressure: Does this team see its own patterns clearly? Can it have the conversation it has been avoiding? Is it making decisions from strategic clarity — or from organizational noise?
>
> ATTUNE-based team work is not generic facilitation. It is a structured diagnostic process that surfaces the specific dynamics creating the most drag — and builds the capacity to change them.

### ALIGNMENT INTENSIVES

**Tag (small, gold):** For teams with a known friction point

> A concentrated working session — half-day or full-day — designed to resolve a specific misalignment, reset accountability, or rebuild the trust that execution depends on.
>
> This is not a planning offsite. It is a structured intervention with a defined outcome: the team leaves with clear commitments, named decision rights, and the conversations that were not happening — happening.
>
> **Best for:** A leadership team navigating a transition, a reorg, a breakdown in cross-functional trust, or a moment where execution has stalled and the cause is relational.

**Text link CTA:**
> About Alignment Intensives →

---

### STRATEGIC LEADERSHIP COUNCIL

**Tag (small, gold):** For senior leaders who need a confidential thinking room

> A small, structured peer advisory forum for senior leaders operating under complexity and consequence. Members convene quarterly to examine real business tensions, pressure-test decisions, and surface the blind spots that are difficult to see from inside the organization.
>
> Every session is facilitated through the ATTUNE framework — which means discussions move past peer opinion into diagnostic rigor. The result is sharper judgment, not just solidarity.
>
> **Best for:** Senior leaders for whom the weight of consequential decisions is isolating — and who need a disciplined, confidential space to think clearly before acting.

**Text link CTA:**
> About the Strategic Leadership Council →

---

### BESPOKE RETREATS

**Tag (small, gold):** For executive teams that need a full reset

> A focused, high-stakes working retreat designed to recalibrate trust, reset operating norms, and restore the collective accountability that sustained performance requires.
>
> Grounded in The Five Behaviors framework — Trust, Conflict, Commitment, Accountability, Results — and facilitated through ATTUNE, these retreats do not produce slides and good intentions. They produce behavioral change and clear commitments that outlast the offsite.
>
> **Best for:** An executive team where trust has thinned, where the same patterns keep surfacing despite structural changes, or where a major transition has exposed fractures that strategy alone will not repair.
