## SECTION 2 — WHAT IT IS

*Design: Light background. Single column, prose. Brief — the visitor wants specifics, not a re-explanation of the problem.*

> An Alignment Intensive is not a planning session. It is not a team-building event. It is a structured intervention with one job: surface the friction that is slowing the team down and leave with clear ownership of what changes next.
>
> Every session is built around the specific tension the team is navigating — not a generic agenda applied from the outside. The ATTUNE framework is the diagnostic lens throughout: what is being avoided, what is going unsaid, where accountability has become ambiguous, and what the team needs to be able to do that it currently cannot.
>
> The work is direct. The output is specific. And the commitments made in the room are designed to hold past the day itself.

## SECTION 3 — HOW IT WORKS

*Design: Navy background. Three phases — Before, During, After — numbered sequence. Each fades in on scroll.*

**Section label (small caps, gold):**
> HOW IT WORKS

**Phase 1 — Before**
> A structured intake conversation with Claudia. The team's situation, the specific friction, what has already been tried. The session agenda is built from this — not from a template.

**Phase 2 — During**
> A half-day or full-day working session, on-site or facilitated remotely. Structured dialogue, not open discussion. The ATTUNE framework shapes how the conversation moves — from surface tension into the dynamics beneath it, then into clear decisions and named ownership.

**Phase 3 — After**
> A written summary of commitments, decision rights, and agreed next steps. Not a slide deck — a working document the team can hold each other to.

## SECTION 6 — WHO THIS IS FOR

*Design: Light grey background. Self-selection signal — the right buyer reads this and recognises their situation.*

**Section label (small caps, gold):**
> IS THIS THE RIGHT FIT?

> An Alignment Intensive works best when:

- A leadership team is navigating a transition, restructuring, or change in scope and needs to reset how it operates
- A specific cross-functional breakdown is creating execution drag and the source is relational, not structural
- Trust between team members or between the team and leadership has thinned — not catastrophically, but enough to show in the work
- A team has been through a difficult period and needs to recalibrate accountability and shared standards before the next phase
- Something in the room is clearly costing the organisation but nobody has yet named it directly

> If the situation is less defined — if the team knows something is off but cannot identify where — the ATTUNE Team Diagnostic is the better starting point.
