## SECTION 2 — WHAT IT IS

*Design: Light background. Two columns — left is copy, right is a stylized preview of the diagnostic PDF.*

> The A.T.T.U.N.E.™ Leadership Diagnostic is a structured self-assessment across six dimensions of leadership behavior under pressure.
>
> For each dimension, you rate yourself honestly: Strength, Developing, or Growth Edge. No performance. No right answers. Just an accurate picture of where you are.
>
> The result is a clear map of where your leadership is reliable, where it is still developing, and where pressure is creating patterns that cost you — in trust, in execution, or in the relationships your work depends on.

## DESIGN NOTES

- **Minimal nav is intentional** — logo + Book a Conversation only. No full nav links.
