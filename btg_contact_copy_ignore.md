## BUILD NOTES

- **No form needed.** Calendly handles scheduling. Direct email handles everything else. A contact form adds friction with no benefit for this ICP.
- **Calendly inline embed** — not popup, not redirect. The visitor books without leaving the page.
- **Phone number** — clickable on mobile (`tel:` link).
- **Email** — clickable (`mailto:` link).
- **LinkedIn** — opens in new tab.
- **Page should be the fastest-loading page on the site.** No heavy images, no animations. The visitor is ready to act.
